The problem behind the problem

AI confusion is usually systems confusion.

A company may think it needs an AI strategy when it actually has disconnected information, unclear ownership, a workflow held together by manual compensation, or a decision no one is authorized to make.

Technology can amplify a healthy system. It can also make a broken one move faster. My role is to help leadership distinguish between those conditions before money, credibility, and employee attention are spent.

How the work proceeds

Understand. Choose. Design. Govern.

The engagement is organized around the result the organization needs—not around a predetermined technology.

  1. 01

    Understand the work

    Listen to leadership and the people doing the work. Map the objective, friction, information, handoffs, and consequence.

  2. 02

    Choose the intervention

    Determine whether the answer is a process change, an existing product, integration, automation, custom tool, decision structure, or no technology at all.

  3. 03

    Design the system

    Define who uses it, what it must know, how it fits the workflow, where people remain in control, and how success will be observed.

  4. 04

    Govern the motion

    Sequence implementation, define ownership and boundaries, and keep the organization from confusing activity with value.

When leadership needs continuity

Independent leadership advisory

Ongoing guidance for executives navigating AI investments, product and vendor choices, pilots, workflow redesign, human oversight, or a portfolio of difficult decisions.

The role

An advisor on the organization’s side of the table—helping leadership ask better questions, recognize hidden dependencies, and decide what deserves to move.

A strong fit

Organizations with capable people and consequential work

  • Leadership knows AI or technology matters but lacks a grounded priority
  • Employees are carrying repeated friction or manual compensation
  • Technical work is strong but decisions still stall
  • The cost of choosing the wrong system is material

Not the right fit

A predetermined tool looking for justification

  • The decision has already been made and only validation is wanted
  • The goal is an AI demonstration without a business problem
  • No one can provide access to the people doing the work
  • There is no owner for the outcome after the recommendation
