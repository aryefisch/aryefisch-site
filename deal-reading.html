The problem

Most stalled deals don't die from a no.

They stall on something nobody said out loud: a decider who isn't in the room, a yes that wasn't a commitment, a risk the buyer won't name. You can feel it. You can't see it.

How it works

Send it. We read it. You get the move.

  1. 01

    Send it

    A call recording, a transcript, or an email thread. Forward it. There is nothing to install and nothing for your team to learn.

  2. 02

    We read it

    Who is actually deciding, what moved, what is holding, and where the deal is heading.

  3. 03

    You get the move

    Within 24 hours: what is really happening, and the one thing to do next.

What you receive

A clear read, every deal, every week.

  • A plain-language read of what is really happening in the deal
  • The one next move, and what to say
  • What to listen for on the next call
  • A running picture of each deal as it moves
