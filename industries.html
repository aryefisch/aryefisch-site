Enhanced Oil Recovery

EOR decisions are motion problems, not just technical ones. CO₂ supply thresholds, pilot scope, capital exposure, reservoir uncertainty — the hard part isn't the model, it's knowing what's allowed to move, when the window closes, and who owns the risk if it's wrong.

What this does for EOR teams

  • Map the authority node and the decision window before capital moves
  • Gate capital exposure and pilot scope against a defined risk boundary
  • Route stalled projects — drift, orbit, backlash, freeze — to the correct correction
  • Turn reservoir uncertainty into the next responsible motion, not another study
